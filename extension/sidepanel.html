PromptVault Post Analyzer

How this works

  • Nothing is read until you click Analyze this post.
  • Then PromptVault reads that one post's text and images. For a reel, it plays the reel once and listens to it.
  • Images and audio are processed and then thrown away. Only the extracted text and the post link are stored, so the next person gets the result instantly.
